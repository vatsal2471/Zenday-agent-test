# Zenday — Customer Research: Website & AI Feature Priorities

*April 2026. Research conducted from the perspective of a firm owner evaluating Zenday.*

---

## 1. THE FIRM OWNER'S BUYING JOURNEY

A firm owner who lands on Zenday's website is NOT casually browsing. They're in pain. Based on the research:

- They worked last weekend on close. They're burned out (99% of accountants experience burnout).
- They tried to hire a junior and couldn't (73-day average time-to-fill, if they can fill it at all).
- They have an offshore team that just turned over, or they're managing one and it's eating their time.
- They may have tried Botkeeper (which just shut down in Feb 2026) or Bench (collapsed Dec 2024) and got burned.
- They've heard about AI bookkeeping but are skeptical. "AI does your bookkeeping" now triggers alarm bells.

**They have 3 questions in their head when they land:**
1. "Does this solve MY specific pain?" (not abstract benefits — my close, my clients, my weekends)
2. "Can I trust this?" (after Bench/Botkeeper, trust is earned, not claimed)
3. "Can I try it without switching anything?" (they're NOT going to migrate off Karbon or Double for a promise)

**They will decide within 10 seconds whether to stay or bounce.** The hero section must answer all three questions in that window.

## 2. COMPETITOR WEBSITE TEARDOWN

### What each competitor says on their homepage:

**Double (formerly Keeper)**
- Headline: "Month-end Close Management Software"
- Pitch: "One platform to communicate with clients, catch coding errors, manage receipts, streamline workflows"
- Key features promoted: email client, QBO/Xero read-write integration, practice management dashboard, receipt management with AI, Chrome extension for QBO
- Social proof: firm testimonials, "best part is it keeps me accountable without input from my side"
- CTA: "Get started" / demo
- **What works:** Clear about what it is (close management). Shows the dashboard. Mentions specific workflows.
- **What doesn't work:** Positioned as a TOOL, not an OUTCOME. Firm owner thinks "another thing to learn." No mention of weekends saved or hours eliminated. No pricing visible.

**Client Hub**
- Headline: "Modern Accounting Practice Management & Client Portal Software"
- Pitch: Combines practice management (portal, workflow, documents, time tracking) with bookkeeping workflows (automate month-end close, resolve uncategorized transactions)
- Key features: client portal, QBO/Xero integration, AI drafts/reviews, workflow automation
- Pricing: per-user (not per-client), firms report 40%+ savings vs competitors
- **What works:** Per-user pricing is attractive for firms with many clients. AI features are clearly listed. Free QBO integration.
- **What doesn't work:** "Practice management" framing makes it compete with Karbon, not replace the offshore team. AI "drafts and reviews" — doesn't DO the work.

**Booke.ai**
- Headline: "The AI Bookkeeper That Logs Into Your QBO & Xero — Like a Team Member"
- Pitch: "AI processes bank feed, categorizes transactions, matches to invoices, reconciles — before your workday begins"
- Key features: 95% autonomous categorization, OCR, client portal, daily/weekly/monthly scheduling, per-client AI brain (no cross-client sharing), learning from corrections
- Pricing: $20/client (basic) or $50/client (full automation). Transparent. Visible on homepage.
- Social proof: "10,000+ businesses, hundreds of firms"
- CTA: "Start free trial"
- **What works:** CLEAR outcome promise ("before your workday begins"). Transparent pricing. Per-client model makes cost predictable. "Like a Team Member" framing is genius — it positions AI as a hire, not a tool. Learning from corrections = trust-building over time.
- **What doesn't work:** Mixed G2 reviews on reliability. Doesn't include close management, reporting, or client chase. It's a categorization tool, not a full close solution.

**Docyt**
- Headline: "AI-Powered Accounting Automation"
- Pitch: "The only full-stack workflow system with AI architecture for the financial back office"
- Key features: HpAI engine, AI Copilot, expense management, continuous reconciliation, close management, real-time reporting, multi-entity support
- Pricing: Starts at $299/month per entity. Custom quotes. 7-day free trial.
- **What works:** "Full-stack" positioning is differentiated. Multi-entity support is unique. Close management is built in.
- **What doesn't work:** Custom pricing is a trust killer for small firms. "Full-stack workflow system with AI architecture" is jargon — firm owners don't talk like this. Feels enterprise, not approachable. $299/entity is expensive for a small firm with 20 simple clients.

**Karbon**
- Headline: "AI-Powered Practice Management for Accounting Firms"
- Key stats promoted: "33,000+ accounting professionals, 34 countries, 18.5 hours saved per week per user"
- AI agents: Bookkeeper, Fractional CFO, Email Triage, Billing, Onboarding
- Aider integration: automated period close, AI management reports, advisory assistant
- CTA: Demo/trial
- **What works:** Massive social proof (33,000 users). "18.5 hours saved per week" is a specific, believable number. Agent framing is modern. Aider integration adds real close capabilities.
- **What doesn't work:** Still a practice management tool with AI bolted on. Agents are inside Karbon's UI, not autonomous. Firm must already use Karbon to benefit.

Show the math explicitly:

## 6. AI FEATURES TO FOCUS ON — RANKED BY FIRM OWNER VALUE

Based on what competitors offer, what firm owners complain about, and what actually drives adoption, here's the priority stack:

### P0 — MUST SHIP IN V1 (without these, the product doesn't exist)

**1. AI Transaction Categorization**
What it does: Pulls bank transactions (via Plaid or QBO API), categorizes them using learned rules + Claude, pushes categorized transactions back to QBO/Xero.
Why it matters: This is the #1 time sink for bookkeepers. 62% of accountant time is spent on compliance tasks like this. Booke.ai's entire product is this one feature.
Accuracy target: 90%+ on routine transactions in month 1, 95%+ by month 3 (as rules learn).
How Claude helps: Claude can read vendor names, infer categories from transaction descriptions, apply chart-of-accounts context, and handle the long tail of ambiguous transactions that ML-era tools couldn't.

**2. Confidence Scoring + Exception Queue**
What it does: Every AI categorization gets a confidence score (0.0 to 1.0). Transactions above a threshold (e.g., 0.95) are auto-posted or marked as "AI-confirmed." Below threshold → goes to the firm owner's review queue.
Why it matters: This is the TRUST MECHANISM. The #1 objection from firm owners is "what if the AI is wrong?" Confidence scoring answers it: "The AI knows what it doesn't know, and it asks you." This is what Botkeeper DIDN'T have — their AI was a black box.
What the firm owner sees: "47 transactions coded. 3 need your review. Click to review."

**3. Multi-Client Daily Status Roster**
What it does: One screen showing every assigned client's state — Current (books up to date), Waiting (pending client response), Review (needs firm owner attention). With dates showing "current as of" for each.
Why it matters: THIS IS THE PRODUCT. Everything else is infrastructure that feeds this one view. The firm owner opens Zenday once per day, sees this screen, and knows exactly what needs their attention across ALL clients. No other tool shows this as a unified real-time view.
What makes it different from Karbon's dashboard: Karbon shows tasks/workflows. Zenday shows OUTCOMES (is this client's books current or not). Tasks are inputs; currency is the output.

**4. One-Click Exception Review**
What it does: For each flagged transaction, show: the transaction details, the AI's suggested category (with confidence score), the reasoning, and three actions — Approve, Override (pick different category), Ask Client (draft a question).
Why it matters: The review flow IS the firm owner's daily workflow. If this takes 45 minutes, the product fails. If it takes 5 minutes across 15 clients, the product wins. Every click must be intentional and minimal.
Design principle: The review flow should feel like approving transactions in a banking app — swipe right to approve, swipe left to override. Speed is everything.

**5. Bank Reconciliation**
What it does: Matches categorized transactions to bank balance. Flags discrepancies. Auto-reconciles what matches perfectly. Shows reconciliation status per client.
Why it matters: Reconciliation is the "close" in "close the books." Categorization without reconciliation is incomplete. The firm owner needs to see "books are reconciled through yesterday" — not just "transactions are categorized."
Technical note: Zenday's Plaid integration gives direct bank balance access, which most competitors don't have. This is a real advantage for automated reconciliation.

---

### P1 — SHIP WITHIN 30 DAYS OF V1

**6. Client Chase Automation**
What it does: When docs are missing or questions are unanswered after X days, AI drafts a chase message (email or portal notification). Firm owner approves with one click. Chase follows an escalating cadence (day 3, day 7, day 14).
Why it matters: 30-40% of close delays are caused by waiting for client responses. This is the #2 time sink after categorization. Karbon+Aider already does this. Booke.ai has a partial version. NOT having this makes continuous close impossible — because the "waiting" status will dominate the roster.
Why AI is critical here: Claude can draft contextually appropriate chase messages — "Hi Sarah, we're missing the receipt for the $2,340 charge at Office Depot on March 15. Could you upload it to your portal or reply with a photo?" This is not a template — it's context-aware, client-specific communication.

**7. Vendor/Rule Learning**
What it does: When the firm owner overrides an AI categorization, the system learns: "DoorDash → Meals & Entertainment" for this client. Next time, auto-categorize with high confidence. Rules are per-client (different clients may categorize differently).
Why it matters: This is how the product gets BETTER over time — the moat that makes switching away painful. Booke.ai claims this ("continuous self-improvement"). Zenday must match it on day 1.
The compound effect: Month 1: 90% accuracy, 10% review. Month 3: 96% accuracy, 4% review. Month 6: 99% accuracy, 1% review. The product becomes invisible — which is the goal.

**8. Receipt Matching**
What it does: Client uploads receipt (via portal, email, or mobile photo). AI reads the receipt using vision, matches it to the corresponding transaction, marks as documented.
Why it matters: Receipt management is a top-5 pain point for every firm. Double prominently features it. Docyt's expense management includes it. Booke.ai's OCR handles it. It's table stakes for any close product.
Claude's advantage: Claude's vision capabilities can read crumpled, blurry, multi-language receipts that traditional OCR struggles with. This is a real differentiator in accuracy.

---

### P2 — SHIP WITHIN 60 DAYS OF V1

**9. Monthly Close Deliverable Generation**
What it does: On the 1st of the month (or whenever the firm wants), auto-generate a financial package for each client: P&L, balance sheet, cash flow statement. Firm owner reviews, adds notes, and sends to client with one click.
Why it matters: The deliverable IS what the firm's client pays for. If Zenday generates it automatically, the firm owner's entire monthly workflow becomes: review roster → handle exceptions → send packages. Nothing else.

**10. Activity Feed / Audit Trail Per Client**
What it does: Detailed chronological log of everything the AI did for each client: transactions coded (with confidence scores), emails read, chase messages sent, questions asked, exceptions flagged, reconciliation completed.
Why it matters: Audit trail is both a trust mechanism and a compliance requirement. Firm owners need to prove to their clients (and potentially regulators) that the work was done correctly. The audit trail also helps the firm owner understand WHY the AI made a specific decision — building trust over time.

**11. Accrual Management**
What it does: For recurring accruals (rent, depreciation, insurance, deferred revenue), AI posts proportional entries daily or monthly based on predefined rules. Month-end adjustments become automatic.
Why it matters: Accruals are one of the last "manual" steps in close that even automated tools don't handle. If Zenday automates accruals, the "continuous" in "continuous close" becomes truly complete — no month-end adjustments needed.

---

### P3 — SHIP WITHIN 90 DAYS OF V1

**12. Advisory Prep / Variance Analysis**
What it does: AI generates month-over-month comparison, flags unusual variances, prepares talking points for client advisory meetings. "Revenue up 12% MoM. COGS up 23% — driven by $8K increase in contractor spend. Recommend reviewing contractor agreements."
Why it matters: This is the "advisory" upgrade that firms are trying to sell to clients at higher price points. If Zenday generates this automatically, the firm can offer advisory services without doing the analytical work.

**13. Multi-Period Trend Detection**
What it does: AI spots anomalies across months — missing recurring expenses, unusual spikes, new vendors, seasonal patterns diverging from prior years.
Why it matters: Catches errors that single-month review misses. "Client usually has $5K in rent every month. This month: $0. Did they move?" This is senior-accountant-level judgment that Claude can approximate.

**14. Firm-Wide Analytics Dashboard**
What it does: Dashboard showing firm-level metrics: total clients under management, average days to close, override rate trend, hours saved per month, AI accuracy over time.
Why it matters: Helps the firm owner quantify the value of Zenday to themselves, their team, and their clients. Also useful for selling Zenday's value to other firm owners (word-of-mouth).

## 8. THE DEMO / TRIAL FLOW

Based on what converts firm owners (from competitor research):

**Step 1: Self-serve signup** (email + firm name)
No sales call. No "request a demo." Firm owners are busy and skeptical. Let them try it on their own terms.

**Step 2: Connect QBO/Xero** (OAuth, 2 clicks)
Table stakes. Every competitor has this. Must be frictionless.

**Step 3: Select one client** (from their QBO client list)
Zenday shows their client list. They pick one. The messiest one. The one they dread.

**Step 4: Zenday starts working** (immediate feedback)
Within minutes: "Pulling 3 months of transaction history... Building vendor dictionary... Learning categorization patterns..."
Within 1 hour: "128 historical transactions analyzed. Vendor rules created for 34 vendors. Ready to start processing new transactions."
Next morning: "12 new transactions categorized overnight. 2 need your review."

**Step 5: First review** (the "aha moment")
The firm owner opens the review queue. Sees 2 flagged transactions with confidence scores, AI reasoning, and one-click approve/override buttons. They review both in under 2 minutes.

**This is the moment they decide to stay or leave.** If the review was fast, accurate, and transparent, they're hooked. If it was confusing, wrong, or slow, they're gone.

**Step 6: Day 5 check-in**
Zenday shows: "Your client [name]'s books are current through yesterday. 47 transactions processed this week. Override rate: 4.2%. Next: we'll draft a chase email for the missing receipt from March 15."

**Step 7: Day 30 conversion**
"Your free trial ends in 3 days. Here's what Zenday did this month: 189 transactions coded, 12 client questions sent, books current every single morning. Continue at $X/month?"

## 9. WHAT MAKES ZENDAY'S WEBSITE DIFFERENT FROM EVERY COMPETITOR

### The messaging DNA:

**Every competitor says:** "We help you manage close / do bookkeeping / automate workflows"
**Zenday says:** "Your books are done. Every morning. You just approve."

**Every competitor shows:** Feature grids, integration logos, pricing tiers
**Zenday shows:** The roster view. One screenshot. "This is your morning now."

**Every competitor's CTA:** "Request a demo" / "Get started" / "Book a call"
**Zenday's CTA:** "Connect your first client in 5 minutes. Free for 30 days."

**Every competitor avoids:** The Bench/Botkeeper graveyard, the trust question, the "what if AI is wrong" concern
**Zenday addresses head-on:** "We don't hide humans behind AI. Here's exactly what it did and why. You approve everything."

### The emotional differentiation:

The firm owner's deepest fear is not "will the AI categorize correctly?" — it's **"will I lose control?"** Every competitor either ignores this fear or tries to minimize it ("don't worry, AI is great!").

Zenday does the opposite: **"You have MORE control than you do now."** With an offshore team, you can't see what they're doing until they deliver. With Zenday, you see EVERYTHING the AI did, in real time, with reasoning attached. The AI is more transparent than any human employee.

This is the emotional flip: from "AI takes control away from me" to "AI gives me MORE control than I've ever had."

## 10. SUMMARY — TOP 5 THINGS TO BUILD FOR THE WEBSITE

1. **The roster view** — one screenshot that IS the product. Green/yellow/red. Multi-client. Current-to-yesterday. This communicates more than any words.

2. **The review queue** — show what the daily 5-minute review actually looks like. Confidence scores. One-click approve. AI reasoning visible. This is what earns trust.
