Is Zenday software or a service?
Software. Your firm assigns Zenday to each client; you stay the accountant of record. Zenday handles the coding, reconciliation, and close — your team reviews anything that needs judgment.
How is this different from Karbon or Client Hub?
Karbon is practice management — Zenday is a working accountant. We do the bookkeeping work and bring it to your roster. Karbon organizes the work you still have to do yourself.
What if Zenday gets something wrong?
Low-confidence items wait in the review queue instead of being posted. When you correct one, Zenday remembers it for that client — permanently. Override rate drops under two per cent by month three.
Which ledgers and banks are supported?
QuickBooks Online, Xero, and any bank on Plaid or direct feed — 12,000+ US and Canadian institutions. Sage and NetSuite are on the roadmap.
Can I try it on one client first?
Yes. First client is free for thirty days. No credit card. Connect QBO and a bank feed, pick a client, and let Zenday do the month.
What about security?
SOC 2 Type II in progress. Your client data is never used to train models. US-hosted. BAA available on Enterprise.
