Is this a bookkeeping service or software?
Software. Your firm assigns Zenday to each client; you stay the accountant of record. Zenday handles the coding, the reconciliation, and the close — your team reviews anything that needs judgment.
How is this different from QBO's built-in AI?
QBO suggests a category on transactions you review one by one. Zenday is a full staff accountant — it runs continuously, learns per client, writes the client questions for you, and closes the month without anyone driving.
What happens when it gets something wrong?
Low-confidence items wait in the review queue instead of being posted. When you correct one, Zenday remembers it for that client — permanently. Override rate drops under two per cent by month three.
Which ledgers and banks do you support?
QuickBooks Online, Xero, and any bank on Plaid or direct feed — 12,000+ US and Canadian institutions. Sage and NetSuite are on the roadmap.
Can I run it on one client before committing?
Yes. First client is free for thirty days. No credit card. No onboarding call — connect QBO and a bank feed, pick a client, and let Zenday do the month.
What about data security?
SOC 2 Type II in progress. Your client data is never used to train models. US-hosted. BAA available on Enterprise.
