A letter from the founder

I use Zenday on my own firm.

I run a CPA firm. We serve small businesses on QuickBooks — 47 clients, last count. For twelve years I did the same thing every month: stayed up late in the second week to close books. Trained offshore staff who moved on every eighteen months. Built workflow after workflow on top of Karbon and spreadsheets.

The thing I kept noticing was that 80% of the work on any given client was categorization, reconciliation, and chasing receipts. Work that doesn't require a CPA. Work that doesn't even require a human, anymore.

So I built the AI I wanted. It watches the bank feeds. It posts entries as transactions clear. It drafts client messages in my firm's voice. It remembers my corrections and never makes the same mistake twice. And critically, it shows its work — confidence scores on every decision, full audit trail, nothing hidden.

The firm looks different now. My staff accountants do advisory work. I spend Tuesday afternoons on client strategy calls instead of reviewing reconciliations. We doubled our client count without hiring, because the bookkeeping scales without us.

Zenday runs my firm. If it breaks, I'm the first to know — because my firm is the first one on it.
