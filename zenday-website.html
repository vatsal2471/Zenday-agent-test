Transparency

Every AI action is logged, audited, and reversible.

◎

Full audit trail

Every entry includes the AI's reasoning, confidence score, and source data. Trace any posted transaction back to the moment it was categorized and why. Export to CSV anytime.

◈

Confidence on every entry

Nothing auto-posts below 95% confidence. Between 80–95%, it goes to your queue. Below 80%, Zenday drafts a client question. You set the thresholds per client.

◉

Your data in your ledger

Zenday writes to your QuickBooks or Xero — not to its own database. If Zenday shut down tomorrow, your books are intact in QBO/Xero exactly as they were. Nothing locked in.
