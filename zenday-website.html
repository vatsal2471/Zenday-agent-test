Built inside a working CPA firm.

Zenday runs my firm's books. Every night. If something breaks, I'm the first one who knows — because I'm the one closing the books the next morning.

The work of bookkeeping is the easiest work to give to an AI. Categorization. Reconciliation. Chasing missing documents. These are pattern-matching tasks that a good model handles overnight, on every client, with full transparency on every decision.

Every feature ships because it made my own Tuesday morning better. If it doesn't, it doesn't ship.
