Transparency, by design

Every AI action is logged, audited, and reversible.

Firms don't trust books they can't defend. Neither do we. Every entry Zenday makes comes with its reasoning, its confidence, and a one-click undo.

◎

Full audit trail

Every entry includes the AI's reasoning trace, confidence score, and source data. Trace any transaction back to the moment it was categorized and why. Export to CSV anytime.

◈

Confidence-gated posting

Nothing auto-posts below 95% confidence. Between 80–95%, it goes to your queue. Below 80%, Zenday drafts a client question. You set the thresholds per client.

◉

Your data, in your ledger

Zenday writes to your QuickBooks or Xero — not to a proprietary database. If Zenday disappeared tomorrow, your books are intact exactly as they were. Nothing locked in.
