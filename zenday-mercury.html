Questions

Answers.

Is this a bookkeeping service or software?
Software. Zenday is the agent your firm assigns to each client. You stay the accountant of record. The agent does the coding, reconciliation, and close — your team reviews the exceptions.
How is this different from QBO's built-in AI?
QBO suggests categories on transactions you review one-by-one. Zenday is an autonomous agent that works 24/7, learns per client, drafts client questions, and closes the month without a human driving.
What if it gets something wrong?
It queues low-confidence items for review instead of guessing. When you correct it, the rule becomes permanent for that client. Override rate drops below 2% by month 3.
Which ledgers and banks?
QuickBooks Online, Xero, and any bank that supports Plaid or direct feed (12,000+ US and CA institutions). Sage and NetSuite on roadmap.
Who sees what the agent does?
Your team. Every entry is traceable to the exact event, reasoning, and model version. The client sees only what you choose to share.
Data security?
SOC 2 Type II in progress. Customer data is not used for model training. Fully US-hosted. BAA available on Enterprise.
